HR Interview Prep: The Hot Seat

Interactive Interview Practice for MBA Students

1. Talent Acquisition
"Since you were a Career Counsellor, how is 'selling a course' similar to 'selling a job'?"

The Winning Answer: "They are both about fit and listening. In counseling, I listened to a student's aspirations to find the right course. In recruitment, I listen to a candidate's career goals to see if they align with the company's vision. If the fit isn't there, 'selling' it will only lead to attrition later."

"What are the key metrics you would track as a recruiter?"

The Winning Answer: "I would track Time to Fill (speed), Cost per Hire (budget), and most importantly, Quality of Hire (retention rates after 6 months)."

2. Learning & Development
"How do you justify the budget for a training program?" Hard

The Winning Answer: "I would use the Kirkpatrick Model. I wouldn't just measure if people 'liked' the training (Level 1). I would measure if their behavior changed (Level 3) and if it impacted business results (Level 4), like increased sales or reduced errors."

3. Compensation & Benefits
"Explain the difference between CTC and Gross Salary." Must Know

The Winning Answer: "CTC (Cost to Company) is the total amount the company spends on an employee, including PF contributions, insurance, and gratuity. Gross Salary is the amount payable before tax deductions. Net Salary (In-hand) is what lands in the bank account after taxes and PF deductions."

Tip: Always mention that CTC is an 'Expense' concept for the company, while Gross is an 'Income' concept for the employee.

4. Employee Relations & Law
"What is the POSH Act?"

The Winning Answer: "It stands for the Prevention of Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act (2013). It mandates that any organization with 10+ employees must have an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) to address grievances."

5. Generative AI in HR
"How can AI assist in Recruitment, and what is the risk?"

The Winning Answer: "AI helps by automating screening, drafting JDs, and scheduling interviews. However, the major risk is Algorithmic Bias—if the AI is trained on historical data that favored one demographic, it might unfairly reject diverse candidates."

6. Teams & Change Management
"Employees hate the new software we installed. How do you fix this?"

The Winning Answer: "I would apply the ADKAR Model. Specifically, I’d focus on 'Awareness' (why we need the change) and 'Ability' (do they actually know how to use it?). Usually, resistance comes from fear of the unknown or lack of training."

7. Downsizing & Politics
"How do you lay off an employee with dignity?" Critical

The Winning Answer: "The conversation must be direct but empathetic. I would ensure they understand the decision is final (to avoid false hope), explain the severance package clearly, and if possible, offer Outplacement Services (help finding a new job) to show we still care about their future."
